The real Oracle handler maps resolve/retry/claim/duplicate/stale to the first five model actions; its stale selector additionally challenges post-oracle sync (no valid model action). The two real rows intentionally have no winning Tier1 proposer, while winner-paid behavior is covered by the separate BondEscalation suites. Healthy callback success, deliberate deferral and both late-API rejection windows are observed; sticky failure checks survive swallowed action reverts. trustedOverrideControl is a deterministic exclusion control. Its independent rows store oracle-first/stale-first expectations and participant/bond baselines. The real Fee handler maps milestone/retryFee/exit to Charge/RetryFee/Settle/Refund. Its two deposits, independent cumulative-gross/fee-policy ghosts, participant baselines, raw remaining and collector/archive/kernel balances establish conservation. Milestone and disputed-exit actions make fresh charges after setup. Four fee-recipient/archive availability combinations cover successful forwarding, fallback and kernel retention. Counters use per-action sink balance deltas, with exact expected counts (two fresh actions for provider-win/split, one for other terminal modes) and zero incompatible counters. No downstream transfer is counted twice. Deterministic tests assert each required branch counter; fail_on_revert=false in stateful tests is never the only completion evidence.

All relevant production writers are covered by the four financial contracts and archive source in coverage.json; interfaces, test fixtures, tool scripts and the inventory itself are also hash-bound. Non-financial registry administration is tested separately, not silently represented by a financial model. Ordinary proposal/finalize bounty mechanics, AI verification, UMA's remote implementation, treasury distributions after receipt and unrelated protocol invariants are outside this skill.
